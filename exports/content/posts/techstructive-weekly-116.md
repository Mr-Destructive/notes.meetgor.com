<h2>Week #116</h2>
<p>Well, it was kind of a boring week. No collegues to talk to, everyone on leave. Sigh! But it was Dusherra week. I read and learnt a lot about Ramayana. Completed a book of Ramayana from Sita’s perspective, Sitayan, Forest of Enchantment whatever you call it. It was beautiful.</p>
<p>I will soon raise a blog for my review on reading books. I already have 20 books read this year, still 2 months to go!</p>
<p>I was realizing this week that software might be solved, but engineering is not. Like I am still not seeing people’s perspective on software change right? It means people are not going to learn software engineering, rather it will make it harder to learn anything in the future, because we will be focused on the outcome and not the process.</p>
<p></p>
<h3>Quote of the Week</h3>
<blockquote>
<p>“We must understand and respect the values that drive our loved ones. We must want what they want, not what we want from them”</p>
<p>— <a href="https://www.goodreads.com/quotes/9669455-even-if-we-love-them-with-our-entire-being-even">Chitra Banerjee Divakaruni, The Forest of Enchantments</a></p>
</blockquote>
<p>I will attach a quote that I found reverberating in me throughout the week. What a beautiful way to put it. I was in awe with this. If we love our family or our children, we must let them do what they want and not what we want for them. I think it gets in the way of someone’s desires or the way of attaining liberation. In this context, it was Sita’s lesson when Kaikeyi had banished Ram for her own son’s benefit. Her perception of his son, Bharat was flawed, she thought the good for him, but her “good” was not his “good” right? We can present them our opinions but not force them to do it. It might be called greedy love or haste in making them happy.</p>

<h2>Tech News</h2>
<ul><li>
<p>eMudhra Certificate Exploit</p>
<ul>
<li><p>eMudhra generated 8 weak cryptographic keys for intermediate Certificate Authorities (CAs) on 30th April 2025! Wow, we survived for over 18 months on this?</p></li>
<li>
<p>The weak randomness made the private keys easier to guess or recover, which means anyone can fake HTTPS certificates to any connection that used eMudhra certification authority.</p>
